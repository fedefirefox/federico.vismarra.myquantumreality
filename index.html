About this project

A living notebook for serious curiosity.

Hej, welcome. This site collects research papers and long-form notes written to make technical physics feel more approachable without sanding off the rigor. The guiding idea is that understanding should come with derivation.

The library is organized for students, researchers, and curious readers who want a direct route into quantum physics, optics, and the mathematical language that supports them.
