About

Research & Interests

I am a Project Scientist at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory in the Stanford context, working on quantum physics, experimental methods, and the mathematical foundations that connect theory to observation.

My focus areas include:

  • Light-Matter Interaction: Semiclassical and quantum approaches to understanding how light couples with matter
  • Optics & Lasers: Pulse propagation, waveplates, high-intensity laser physics, and optical diagnostics
  • Quantum Devices: Superconductivity, spintronics, and quantum gates
  • Mathematical Foundations: Complex analysis, imaginary units, and the notation that makes quantum theory precise

This site is an open repository of research notes and papers, built to share science with rigor and accessibility. Every derivation is from first principles.
